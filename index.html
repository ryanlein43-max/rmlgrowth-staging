AI search

What AI search means for local businesses.

A short guide to how AI assistants answer questions about local services, and what you can do about it.

What is AI search?

People now ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for products and local services, and the assistant writes back an answer. That answer often names specific businesses. Generative engine optimization, or GEO, is the work of making your business easier for those tools to find and describe correctly.

Why does it matter for a local business?

An assistant's answer is short, and a customer may only hear about the businesses it names. If the information about your business online is thin or out of date, the assistant has less to work with when it describes you.

How is it different from SEO?

It builds on the same foundation. Accurate listings and a clearly organized website help with both. AI search puts extra weight on how clearly your business is described across the web and whether those descriptions agree with each other. It pairs well with our SEO service.

Can you guarantee that an assistant will recommend my business?

No one can. These tools decide what to say on their own, and their answers change over time. Our job is to make accurate, consistent information about your business easy for them to find. We check how they describe you on a regular schedule and adjust the work based on what we see.
